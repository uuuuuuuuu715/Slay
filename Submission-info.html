This Month

September — The Cave

A starting point, not a requirement. Interpret it literally, philosophically, politically, historically or aesthetically. Take it somewhere strange, argue with it, or ignore it entirely.

Need somewhere to start?

Plato's cave. Shadows. Echo chambers. Underground culture. Hidden histories. Nightlife. Illusion. Secrecy. Subcultures. Things buried, forgotten, concealed or deliberately unseen.

These aren't questions you have to answer — they're rabbit holes. Follow whichever one interests you.

What can I submit?

Essays, philosophy, history, politics, cultural criticism, fashion writing, reviews, poetry, short fiction, art, photography and work that doesn't fit neatly into any of those.

We especially like pieces that collide different subjects: philosophy and fashion, economics and religion, history and music, politics and art, literature and internet culture.

The Rules

Your submission must be your own original work. Credit quotations, images and ideas that aren't yours, and include sources where appropriate.

Please submit your work using a Google Docs or Google Drive link and make sure the sharing setting allows anyone with the link to view it.

You can include a note with your submission explaining the piece, pitching an idea, giving image credits, or telling us anything else we should know.
